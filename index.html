About

Hi! I am Haoran, an MS student at the Machine Learning Department of Carnegie Mellon University. Previously, I was fortunate to work with Prof. Alan Yuille at Johns Hopkins University and Prof. Huaxiu Yao at UNC-Chapel Hill. I am interested in building intelligent agents that can learn from rich contexts and reason over long horizons to execute complex tasks.
